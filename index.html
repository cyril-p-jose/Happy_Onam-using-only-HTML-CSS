The Legend of Mahabali & Onam

"മാവേലി നാടു വാണീടും കാലം,
മാനുഷരെല്ലാരും ഒന്നുപോലെ..."

According to legend, King Mahabali's reign over Kerala was an era of unmatched prosperity, truthfulness, equality, and joy, where no sorrow or deceit existed.

Granted a boon to visit his beloved people once every year during the Malayalam month of Chingam, the people of Kerala celebrate his annual homecoming with vibrant Pookkalams, sumptuous Onasadya feasts, Vallamkali boat races, and joyful festivities.
